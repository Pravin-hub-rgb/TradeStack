# Stock vs Nifty — Summary (Rules from concept.md)

## Core Premise (Crux)

- **Nifty 50 koi alag entity nahi** — bas top 50 companies ka average, wo Indian market ko represent karta hai.
- **Nifty upar chal raha hai → saare stocks (small/mid/large cap) chalne lagte hain**, sabko support milta hai. Long-term: major index + top 50 perform → saare stocks mein support.
- **Crux ek line:** Bure samay (Nifty fall) mein jo stock support sustain kar paata hai, wo market recovery pe sabse tezi se bhagega.
- Zaroori premisa: **Nifty kisi bhi day pe forever trend/kame nahi karta**. Trending days ka ratio sirf **10–15–20%** hai (~10 days mein avg 1.5 days trending). **Market nature = swings mein move karna.** Morning mein gir raha hai → support banega + doosra (buying) swing aayega. Yahi edge hai.

## Outperform Definition

- Stock kam girega → usmein support dekhne ko milega → Nifty se zyada recovery → zyada positive. Iska matlab outperform hai.

## Rules

### 1. Universe

- **Method (comparison strategy):** Nifty ko kisi bhi Nifty 50 stock se compare karo. Focus un stocks par jo Nifty ka major weightage carry karte hain (bade, liquid).
- Sirf equities (intraday) — safest, beginners + loss-makers ke liye.
- Nifty 50 ke bade liquid stocks — SBI, HDFC Bank, Reliance (jo Nifty ka major weightage carry karte hain).

### 2. Strong vs Weak

- **Strong aur weak stocks ek saath present ho sakti hain (kisi bhi segment — large/mid/small mein), independent of Nifty ke direction.** Nifty bhale hi buying side ja raha ho, dono type market mein hoti hain.
- Strong = accumulation (FII/DII/MF buying, achha result, positive news). Weak = poor fundamentals.
- Strong stock is day pe kam girega / support sustain karega jab Nifty huge fall raha ho. (Nuance: strong stock bhi Nifty ke aggressive sell-off mein thoda bahut impact hokar gir sakta hai — par wo hi uthne wala stock hota hai.)
- **Weak stock rule:** Nifty agar market ko support karega to weak stock bhi girne ka hoga to support sustain karega / utna nahi girega. **Lekin agar Nifty SAME time pe girne laga to weak stock sabse pehle sabse tezi se girega.**

### 3. Small Cap Vulnerability (universe logic)

- Market crash → small caps sabse zyada girte. Market upar → small caps sabse zyada bhagte (high-beta).
- Reason: chhoti kashti pehle doobti hai (fundamentally weak, unstable business, small market cap).

### 4. Setup / Timing Filters

- Sirf tab watch karo jab **Nifty significantly neeche gira ho in FIRST selling swing** → kyunki uske baad doosre swing mein **buying aane ka zyada probability**.
- **Filter:** Agar Nifty sirf 1–2 candle gira aur bounce aa gaya → **NOT high probability, skip**.
- "Reversal aa sakta hai" wale Nifty point ka wait karo (market nature ke hisaab se).

### 5. Strength Identification (candle-by-candle, Nifty vs Stock)

- **Thesis:** Agar pura market average girne ke baad bhi stock nahi gira → iska matlab usmein kuch khaas baat hai (strength/accumulation) → **usi stock ko pakadna hai**.
- List of strong stocks = wahi jo Nifty girte hue support sustain karein. Inference: *Intraday analysis sirf technical se hota hai (fundamental nahi).*
- Nifty **previous candle ka low break** kare, stock **NAHI kare** → support held (sign 1).
- Buying candle aaye: Nifty ki **weak** buying, stock ki **full engulfing/consuming** candle → strength sign (sign 2).
- Nifty lagatar **lows ki taraf proceed** kare, stock **sirf 1 red candle** dekar **intraday high ke kareeb close** kare → strength candle (sign 3). Seller doosri baar push nahi kar paya.
- **Same-time low divergence:** Nifty aur stock same time low lagaye (e.g. 10:40), lekin fir Nifty low break kare aur stock NAHI → strength.
- Stock **outperform** kare (kam gira, support sustain, zyada recovery, zyada positive).
- Jahan se Nifty reversal aa sakta hai wahin wait karo — agar stock mein strength hai to wo **dugni tezi se bhagega** aur **healthy breakout** dekhne ka zyada probability hota hai.

### 6. Price Bhi Ek Signal

- Achha stock jis din sasta ho jaye → buyer ke liye lucrative, buyer jaldi buying karta hai.

### 7. Volume Confirmation

- Morn/closing volume = FII/DII normal execution.
- **Mid-day significant volume** = accumulation at important breakout/breakdown zones.
- **Lakhs-crores volume + support hold + stock na girna** = institutional accumulation, retail nahi.

### 8. Unconfirmed Zone

- Major breakout ekdum se nahi aata — pehle accumulation chalta hai. Stock breakout se pehle ek **"unconfirmed zone"** mein time leta hai. Ye zone track karna hai.
- Breakout pehle se "awaited" rehta hai.

### 9. Entry

- **No-trade jab stock sirf gir raha ho. Action sirf tab lena hai jab highs break honge.**
- **Jab stock apna previous high / lower high / resistance break kare** (breakout / healthy breakout).
- Entry **breakout point par** ya **breakout candle ke close par**.

### 10. Stop Loss

- **Breakout candle ka low** (nearest / previous candle ka low).

### 11. Exit / Trailing

- Move ko trail karo, jo bhi aa raha ho capture karo, **reversal pe exit**.

### 12. Edge Principle ("Science Dikhana")

- Bhoochal/tabaahi aane se pehle market **science dikha deta hai** — strength + accumulation **1 din pehle / breakout se pehle** dikh jaati hai. Isi pre-signal ko catch karna hai.
- Ye strategy ka true crux hai: "stock mein strength dikhegi, accumulation dikhega" — breakout, achha result, heavy buying se pehle.
